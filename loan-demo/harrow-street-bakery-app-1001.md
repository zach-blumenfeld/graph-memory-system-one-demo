What I decided and why. Risk tier: medium. On its own record the bakery would be low: coverage of 1.6, a spotless payment history, current KYC, six years with us, and a relationship manager describing a signed lease and growing wholesale trade [cite: NOTE-01]. It is medium because the owner who will run the new site day to day [cite: NOTE-01] is the majority owner of a business that has missed three consecutive payments to us [cite: FAC-03]. Connected exposure: material. Decline is not supported by the bakery's own record and would go to a human anyway under G1 [cite: BUS-01].
