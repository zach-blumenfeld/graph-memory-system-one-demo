Decision: decline. Corvid Print Co. Ltd [cite: BUS-03] applied for a working capital line of 150,000 over 36 months [cite: APP-1003]. The policy check returns an automatic decline on two hard rules: the KYC record is overdue [cite: KYC-BUS-03] and there is a fraud event 4.1 months ago, inside the twelve-month window [cite: FRD-01]. Neither rule leaves room for judgment, so the application is declined.

## What was asked for

The business requested 150,000 as a working capital line over 36 months, with a proposed monthly payment of 4804.94, to fund paper stock and payroll through the seasonal trough [cite: APP-1003]. Corvid Print is a commercial printing business that has banked with Lantern Mutual since May 2023, a tenure of 41 months or 3.4 years [cite: BUS-03]. The application was submitted by the sole owner, who holds 100 percent of the business [cite: PER-05].

## What the policy found

Rule R1, KYC current: the KYC record is overdue. The last review was on 2024-07-02 and beneficial owners are not verified [cite: KYC-BUS-03]. Effect: automatic decline.

Rule R2, no fraud event in twelve months: on 2026-06-10 the fraud team blocked the operating account after counterfeit cheque deposits, 4.1 months before the assessment date [cite: FRD-01]. Effect: automatic decline.

The relationship manager's note from 2026-07-01 records the block and states that a KYC refresh was requested twice with no response from the director [cite: NOTE-04].

The remaining checks were run for completeness and would not on their own have blocked the application:

- Coverage: twelve-month average monthly net inflow of 8206.03 [cite: MON-301-2025-10] [cite: MON-301-2025-11] [cite: MON-301-2025-12] [cite: MON-301-2026-01] [cite: MON-301-2026-02] [cite: MON-301-2026-03] [cite: MON-301-2026-04] [cite: MON-301-2026-05] [cite: MON-301-2026-06] [cite: MON-301-2026-07] [cite: MON-301-2026-08] [cite: MON-301-2026-09] against total monthly payments of 6312.33, being the proposed 4804.94 [cite: APP-1003] plus existing payments of 1507.39 [cite: FAC-06]. Coverage ratio 1.3, above the 1.25 floor.
- Exposure: the existing term loan of 60,000 [cite: FAC-06] plus the requested 150,000 [cite: APP-1003] gives a total exposure of 210,000. Connected exposure is 0, because the owner holds no other business at 25 percent or more [cite: PER-05].
- Connected delinquency: none. Connected worst late days is 0.
- Existing facility conduct: the term loan [cite: FAC-06] shows 0 days late, with every payment from October 2024 through September 2026 made on its due date.
- Tenure: 41 months, above the two-year review threshold and below the five-year threshold that requires a human to see a decline before it is filed [cite: BUS-03].

## What was decided and why

Decline. The two fired rules are hard rules and the write-up does not argue with them. The sound cash flow and the clean payment record on the existing term loan are noted, but they do not offset an unverified ownership record and a fraud event on the operating account four months ago. A counter-offer at a smaller amount is also a credit extension and is barred by the same rules. The case is not referred because tenure is under five years and the policy outcome is unambiguous.

The risk tier is assessed as high, on the basis of the fraud event [cite: FRD-01], the overdue KYC with beneficial owners unverified [cite: KYC-BUS-03], and the unanswered refresh requests [cite: NOTE-04]. Connected exposure is not material to this decision because there is none.

The business may reapply once the KYC refresh is completed and beneficial owners are verified, and once twelve months have passed since the fraud event. On the cash flow figures in the systems, a fresh application at that point would clear the coverage floor.
