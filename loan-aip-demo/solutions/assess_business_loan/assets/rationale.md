## How to write it

- State the outcome in the first sentence, then what was asked for, what the policy found,
  and what was decided and why. Three to five short paragraphs.
- Every figure you quote (a ratio, a payment, an exposure, days late, a tenure) comes from a
  record in the case file; mark it with the record's id, like `[cite: FAC-03]`. Quote no figure
  the case file does not hold.
- Do not argue with a hard rule. Where the outcome is a referral, say what the reviewer should
  establish.
- Describe the business, not the people: no age, date of birth or any personal characteristic
  of an owner.
