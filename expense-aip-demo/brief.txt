Expense claim review. A claim arrives with an id, amount, category, date, claimant, approver, and the receipt as free text, often messy: a photo transcription with the vendor, line items and total somewhere in it. Read the receipt text to find the vendor, the date and the total, and say whether it supports what was claimed and why. Check the claim against our policy: no single item over 200, no more than 1000 a month, only travel, meals, software and equipment, a receipt for anything over 25, no alcohol, submitted within 30 days of the receipt date, the claimant cannot be named as the approver, and no splitting one purchase into several claims. There is no history to look up: judge the monthly limit and splitting from this claim and its receipt alone. If the receipt supports the claim and every rule clearly holds, approve it and append a ledger line (claim id, claimant, amount, category, date, status) to a file in the working directory. If anything is doubtful or fails, a person decides and gives a one-line reason. Status is approved or declined. Return the status and the reason. The approver is the name in the claim's approver field; the self-approval rule holds when it differs from the claimant. The claim's date is the day it was submitted; the 30 days run from the receipt's date to the claim's date. The ledger file is ledger.txt in the working directory. Every decided claim gets a ledger line, approved or declined, whoever decided it. All amounts are in one currency, so a receipt's currency sign or code is never a reason to doubt it.
